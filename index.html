Questions

Why hire me instead of someone cheaper?

Why do you cost more than the average freelancer?

Because you are paying for a shipped product, not hours. Most freelance app projects stall between "it runs" and "it is on the App Store". I do the whole path, design through App Review, and I have done it seventeen times. A cheaper build that never launches is the most expensive option there is.

Who owns the code?

You do. The repository, the design files, the App Store listing and the release pipeline are yours at handover.

Do you work with agencies or other developers?

Yes. I can build a feature, rescue a stuck release, set up automated App Store delivery for your team, or review an existing codebase at the hourly rate.

What do you not do?

Games with heavy 3D art, Android-only apps, and anything I cannot ship to a standard I would put my name on. If it is not a fit, I will say so on the first email and point you somewhere better.

How fast can you start?

Usually within two weeks of a signed scope. Prototype sprints can often start sooner.
