What the analysis is

Decode, per unit

Every broadcast is decoded and graded by remoteid — the published Rust decoder for ASTM F3411 and DJI DroneID — exactly as a receiver would.

Grade, whole field

remoteid-sentinel reads all tracks at once and raises the cross-track signatures one radio hiding behind hundreds of fake identities cannot avoid: fan-out, templated serials, frozen counters, shared pilot positions.

Honest by design

An anomaly is evidence, not identity. The report says detected, not confirmed: it never claims to name a pilot, and it says so on the page.

Receive-only

The stack opens no radio and has no transmit path, anywhere. It reads broadcasts a sensor already heard and returns a verdict, never a frame.

Honest by design. The verdict and the report are produced by the open-source crates themselves, run server-side over the exact bytes you upload. Nothing is simulated on this page except the optional sample capture, which is labelled as generated.

The analyzer and the report grade broadcasts. A spoofed or suspicious grade is evidence about a transmission, not the identification of a person.
